# Decision Model

A defensible engineering decision states:

- the outcome sought;
- the invariants that must hold;
- the evidence and uncertainty;
- credible alternatives, including doing nothing or deleting work;
- expected value, ownership cost, reversibility, and second-order effects;
- the selected option and conditions that would change it;
- verification and rollback.

Decision quality is measured by fitness to reality, not by decisiveness or sophistication.
