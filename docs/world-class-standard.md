# World-Class Standard

World-class is an evidence threshold, not a branding adjective.

A world-class engineering skill must demonstrate:

- one responsibility that materially benefits from procedural guidance;
- precise positive and negative activation boundaries;
- a demonstrated advantage over selecting no skill;
- inputs and authority requirements;
- an evidence, decision, safety, or completion gate;
- proportional behavior for low and high consequence;
- explicit verdicts and stop conditions;
- a useful output contract;
- adjacent responsibilities it refuses to absorb;
- failure patterns derived from observed model behavior;
- positive, negative, restraint, and cross-skill evaluation cases;
- paired improvement over the same model without the skill;
- acceptable token, tool, and time cost;
- near-zero unnecessary activation on routine build, test, verification, container, and deployment actions;
- no material safety, task-success, or routing regression;
- reproducibility across the supported model and agent conditions.

Length, confidence, comprehensiveness, and philosophical language do not establish quality.
Delete or demote any skill that cannot earn its context cost.

The suite is world-class only when its routing and composition outperform both the unskilled baseline and plausible smaller configurations.
No skill should be the most common correct routing result in an effective general engineering environment.
