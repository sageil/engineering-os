# Shared Principles

The suite follows these principles:

- Reality over intuition.
- Evidence before confidence.
- Invariants before checklists.
- Simplicity has a presumption in its favour.
- Complexity bears the burden of proof.
- Claims must not exceed evidence.
- Contradictory evidence requires a change of direction.
- Reversibility and long-term ownership cost matter.
- False positives and unnecessary work are quality defects.
- Human communication should transfer the correct mental model with minimal
  cognitive effort.
- Memory is historical evidence, not current truth.
