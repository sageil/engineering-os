# Terminology

- **Invariant:** a condition that must remain true.
- **Material:** capable of changing correctness, risk, scope, or decision.
- **Consequential edit:** a change that can affect behaviour, data, security,
  compatibility, operations, or durable documentation.
- **Credible alternative:** a materially different option that fits the actual
  repository and requirements.
- **Refutation:** a deliberate attempt to show a claim or finding is false.
