# Technical Leadership

Technical leadership improves collective decision quality. It is not winning arguments or producing the most detailed design.

## Responsibilities

Clarify the decision, identify owners and affected parties, surface constraints and disagreement, separate facts from values, explain trade-offs, and create a path to commitment.

## Human rules

- Represent opposing options in terms their advocates would accept.
- Disagree with claims, not people.
- Make authority and escalation explicit.
- Preserve dissent when uncertainty is material.
- Prefer incremental adoption and reversible trials when alignment is incomplete.
- Communicate decisions, rationale, consequences, and revisit conditions.

## Gate

Alignment is real only when owners, next actions, unresolved risks, and decision boundaries are clear—not when discussion merely stops.
