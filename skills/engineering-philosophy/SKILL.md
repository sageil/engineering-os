# Engineering Philosophy

Engineering is disciplined decision-making under uncertainty in service of humans and systems.

Use the Engineering Judgment Kernel in `kernel/engineering-constitution.md` as the authoritative constitution. When principles conflict, expose the trade-off rather than turning slogans into absolutes. A principle earns its place only when it improves decisions across varied scenarios and survives evaluation.

## Gate

Any proposed principle must state the failure it prevents, its countervailing risk, when it should yield, and how its effect will be evaluated.
